# Research Skill

## Name

research

## Version

1.0.0

## Objective

Produce a traceable answer supported by evidence and explicit verification.

## Inputs

- research question
- constraints
- desired output format

## Procedure

1. Clarify the research question.
2. Break the question into verifiable claims.
3. Identify what evidence is required for each claim.
4. Gather relevant evidence.
5. Separate evidence from inference.
6. Check for contradictions.
7. Record uncertainty where evidence is incomplete.
8. Produce a concise synthesis.
9. Verify the final answer against the original question.

## Failure Conditions

- A material claim has no supporting evidence.
- Evidence and inference are presented as the same thing.
- Conflicting evidence is ignored.
- Uncertainty is hidden.
- The final answer does not address the original question.

## Evaluation Rubric

- Evidence: 35%
- Correctness: 35%
- Traceability: 20%
- Clarity: 10%
